## Hello, laundromat owners!

We built LaundryPing to solve a simple problem: **customers forget to pick up their laundry**. With a single tap, your staff can send a "laundry done" SMS notification — no more phone calls, no more unclaimed loads sitting around.

### How it works

1. **Start a job** — When a customer drops off laundry, your staff logs it in LaundryPing with the customer's phone number.
2. **Complete the job** — When the laundry is done, tap "Complete" and an SMS is sent automatically.
3. **Customer picks up** — The customer gets a friendly Tagalog/English text and comes to collect.

### Why SMS?

In the Philippines, SMS is still king. Not everyone has reliable mobile data, but almost everyone can receive a text message. LaundryPing uses the Semaphore API to deliver fast, affordable SMS to any Philippine mobile number.

### What's next

We're just getting started. Expect more features like job tracking, analytics, and self-serve plan management. Stay tuned!
