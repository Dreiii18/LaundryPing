# LaundryPing Queue Features — Facebook Launch Content

## Strategy

Both queue features ship together in **v1.9.3**, but are announced on Facebook as **2 separate posts** spaced 3-5 days apart. This maximizes engagement (2x algorithm chances) while keeping engineering simple (one release).

Customers don't know or care about release dates — they see "new feature!" when you post.

---

## General Posting Guidelines

### Timing
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best time:** 10-11 AM PHT
- **Avoid:** Monday mornings (busy), Friday afternoons (checked out), weekends (lower B2B engagement)
- **Space posts 3-5 days apart** — never post two features the same week

### Engagement
- Reply to ALL comments within 2 hours of posting
- If someone asks a question about a feature, reply with a short demo video or screenshot
- "What feature should we build next?" at the end of every post turns updates into market research
- Pin the most recent feature post to the top of your page

### Voice
- **Tagalog-first** with English sprinkled in (matches how PH business owners actually talk)
- Keep it conversational, not corporate
- Use "niyo" / "namin" / "kayo" — make it feel personal
- Avoid jargon: say "text" not "SMS notification", say "queue" not "pending state"

### Content Mix (weekly)
- 1-2x feature updates (these posts)
- 1x laundromat tip / industry content
- 1x customer success story or testimonial (when available)
- Don't post ONLY features — it feels like an ad feed

### Metrics to Track
- Post reach
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + shares / reach)
- Link clicks
- Comments asking about features (= market research gold)
- New signups within 48 hours of posting
